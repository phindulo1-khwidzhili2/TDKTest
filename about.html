About us

TDK Designs and Branding Solutions is a graphic design and print shop in Louis Trichardt, Limpopo, founded by Thinavhuyo Desmond Mudimeli.

We work with local businesses, organisations and individuals who want to be noticed, giving them strong designs and high-quality printing.

We are a one-stop shop. The same team designs your job, prints it and installs it, so nothing gets lost between the idea and the finished product.
